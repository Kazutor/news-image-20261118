LEAD  •  GULF & MIDDLE EAST

US–Iran war escalates overnight

The US says it sank five Iranian oil tankers in the Gulf of Oman after Iran twice tried to hit a US warship. Iran says it attacked 10 ships near the Strait of Hormuz and fired missiles at a US base in Jordan; Bahrain says it stopped a drone attack on a US radar site.

🌊

Kerch Bridge down; Kyiv bridges hit

Ukraine struck the bridge to Crimea for a second night running, bringing down a road span. Russia hit Kyiv’s North and South bridges and damaged part of the metro.

🌎

Asia diplomacy: Trump in Manila, Xi meets Carney

Trump held the first Quad leaders’ meeting in two years, with India, Japan and Australia, before APEC in Shenzhen. Xi and Carney agreed tariff relief worth about C$400m for Canadian pork and seafood.

🌎

COP31 enters its decisive phase

Climate talks in Antalya, Türkiye, move to ministers as a first draft deal draws fire. 144 disputed items remain, with fossil fuels and finance at the centre. Talks are due to end on Friday.

⛺

Gaza faces winter in flooded tents

The UN warns of more deaths from the cold as storms flood camps. Australia, Canada, Italy and the UK urge Israel to let aid in, with 150,000 tents said to be held at the border.

💼

Israel: Eisenkot asked to form a government

President Herzog has asked Gadi Eisenkot to try to form the next government, passing over Benjamin Netanyahu after the 27 October election left no bloc with a majority. He has 28 days.

🎬

And finally: a new James Bond

Amazon MGM names Robert Pattinson as the next 007, succeeding Daniel Craig. Denis Villeneuve will direct the new film, the first Bond under the studio’s control.
